📶 Easy Hotspot Setup Guide (No Wi-Fi Router Needed)

  1. On this laptop, press Windows Key + I → go to Network & Internet → toggle on Mobile Hotspot.
  2. Have friends connect their phones to your laptop's hotspot Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Host Big Screen on your laptop and have players scan the big on-screen QR Code with their phone cameras!
  4. No phone? Open a second browser window at /play and click + Add Player to switch between multiple players seamlessly!
